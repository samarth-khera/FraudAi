## Limits of this demonstration

- One deterministic example per scenario (plus aggregates over every such account for 4, 5 and 6, and 30 accounts for 10); the broader rates are in `ml/reports/investigation_eval_demo.json` and `investigation_eval_cold.json`.
- The ledger is synthetic and comes from the same generator family the models were trained on (a different seed and population). Behaviour on real bank data is unknown.
- Pass/fail checks encode the owner's expected behaviour; a pass is not proof the engine is right in general.
